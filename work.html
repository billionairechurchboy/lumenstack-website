Concept project
Agriculture · Custom Software · Concept

Harvest Ledger

An inventory and distribution platform for a regional produce distributor.

The problem
Orders arrived by phone, WhatsApp and email. Stock was tracked in spreadsheets, causing over-selling, spoilage and late deliveries.
What we built
A web platform with live inventory, a retailer ordering portal, route planning for drivers and a manager dashboard.
The result
40% fewer stock errors, orders fulfilled three times faster and a single view of the business for the leadership team.

Concept project
Healthcare · Mobile App · Concept

CareLink

A patient booking and reminders app for a network of outpatient clinics.

The problem
Patients waited on hold to book appointments, and one in five appointments was missed with no notice.
What we built
An iOS and Android app for booking, rescheduling and reminders, plus a staff scheduling console connected to the clinics’ existing records system.
The result
35% fewer no-shows, shorter phone queues and a 4.8-star average rating from patients.

Concept project
Logistics · Business Automation · Concept

Shipwise Ops

Automated dispatch, tracking and invoicing for a last-mile delivery company.

The problem
The operations team re-typed parcel data between three systems and built invoices by hand at the end of every week.
What we built
Automated workflows that assign drivers, send customer tracking updates and generate invoices straight from delivery data.
The result
Around 20 hours saved every week, 99.2% invoice accuracy and customers who always know where their parcel is.
