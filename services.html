FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A marketing website may take 4–8 weeks, while a custom platform or mobile app usually runs 3–6 months. We’ll give you a clear timeline after discovery.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. We help founders validate ideas, scope an MVP and launch quickly, then scale the product as the business grows.

Can you improve a system we already have?

Absolutely. Many projects start with an audit of existing software, followed by targeted improvements, integrations or a phased rebuild.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, monitoring and ongoing improvement plans tailored to what your product needs.

Do you work with international clients?

We do. We work remotely with businesses across time zones and adapt our communication rhythm to suit your team.
